One person from sketch to handover

Most projects split between an architect, an interior designer and a contractor who have never met. Rami has spent fifteen years doing all three jobs on the same projects — which is why his buildings feel finished on the inside, and his interiors survive contact with the site.

He has managed construction of towers and villas, fitted out offices and gyms, built shops and exhibition stands on impossible deadlines, and drawn furniture when the room asked for it.
